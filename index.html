The Bates Mansion Experience

More Than a Venue: An Authentic Tucson Destination for Life’s Most Meaningful Gatherings

Nestled in the heart of Downtown Tucson, the Historic Bates Mansion offers a rare blend of timeless Southwestern architecture, warm Old Pueblo charm, and flexible indoor/outdoor event flow. Formerly the home of the famed Mountain Oyster Club, this storied venue has been thoughtfully revitalized to host unforgettable modern celebrations.

From romantic outdoor patio ceremonies framed by towering palm trees to vibrant receptions inside our mood-lit cocktail lounge with its iconic historic bar, every corner of the property breathes character. Coupled with our in-house planning expertise and direct connection to Tucson Event Rentals, we ensure your event planning process is seamless from start to finish.

Legacy & Character

The Story of C.T.R. Bates & The Mountain Oyster Club

Originally associated with prominent Tucson figures including Charles T.R. Bates, 283 N Stone Avenue holds a legendary position in Old Pueblo history. From 1975 until 2004, the mansion served as the downtown headquarters for the famous Mountain Oyster Club—a private social hub for Arizona Southwestern culture, cattlemen, artists, and community leaders.

Today, while fully reimagined for modern weddings and private galas, the mansion retains its historic soul: thick adobe-style walls, classic arches, dark vintage paintings, and a subtle nod to Tucson’s spirited past.

A Touch of Tucson LoreLocals and visitors alike affectionately whisper about the mansion's friendly, historic ghosts. We embrace this playful lore as part of our unique Old Pueblo identity—adding a dash of charming Tucson intrigue to an otherwise sophisticated historic estate.
